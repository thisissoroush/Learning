# Chapter 12: The Information Stock Exchange
## *Managing Information, Secrets, and Communication*

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

**Information is currency in organizations.** You hold sensitive information (salaries, hiring plans, performance issues). How you handle it affects trust, morale, and culture.

---

## 🤐 Types of Information

**Public Information**
- Team roadmaps, completed projects
- Company announcements
- General strategy and direction
- Share freely

**Private Information**
- Performance reviews, issues with specific people
- Salary information
- Confidential business decisions
- Keep strictly confidential

**Sensitive Information**
- Pending layoffs, restructuring
- Acquisition/merger activity
- Strategic pivots or failures
- Share on need-to-know basis

---

## 📢 Communication Principles

**Be Transparent:**
- Share what you can when you can
- Explain why you can't share something
- Give timeline for when you can share
- Don't keep secrets out of spite

**Avoid Information Asymmetry:**
- Don't favor certain people with info
- Team should hear major news from you, not rumors
- Communicate decisions and reasoning
- Consistency matters

**Wrong:**
- Telling one person about job opening before others
- Sharing salary info unevenly
- Spreading rumors or hearsay

---

## 🔗 Information Flow

**Downward Communication**
- Regular updates from leadership
- Clear explanations of decisions
- Context on strategy and direction
- Frequency: weekly minimum

**Upward Communication**
- Regular check-ins with your manager
- Weekly status updates
- Early warnings about problems
- Frequency: consistent

**Lateral Communication**
- Cross-team sync meetings
- Documentation and shared knowledge
- Regular updates to peers
- Helps prevent silos

---

## 📋 Documentation

**Importance:**
- Creates institutional memory
- Reduces dependence on individuals
- Enables asynchronous communication
- Helps onboard new people

**What to document:**
- Major decisions and reasoning
- Process documentation
- Architecture and technical decisions
- Historical context

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Information flow affects culture and trust
- Be transparent; explain constraints
- Don't create information asymmetry
- Communicate regularly in all directions
- Document decisions and reasoning
- Build information sharing into culture
