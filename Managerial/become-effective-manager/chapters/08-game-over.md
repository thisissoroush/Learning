# Chapter 8: Game Over
## *When People Leave and Firing Decisions*

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

**People leaving is normal**. Some exits are good (people ready for new challenges), some are bad (you drove them away), and some involve termination (performance issues). Handle each professionally and with dignity.

---

## 📊 People Leaving Is Normal

Healthy turnover:
- ~10-15% annual turnover is normal
- Keep 80-90% of high performers
- People move on; that's okay
- Your goal: keep great people; let mediocre ones go when better options exist

---

## 👋 When Staff Leave

**Exit Interview Process:**
1. **Give notice period** — Usually 2 weeks; respect their employment agreement
2. **Understand why** — Exit interview; learn what you could improve
3. **Knowledge transfer** — Document their work; train replacement
4. **Team communication** — Explain their departure; plan coverage
5. **Celebrate** — Acknowledge their contributions
6. **Stay connected** — Good employees become future collaborators/referrals

**What to ask in exit interview:**
- Why are they leaving?
- What could we have done better?
- Would they recommend working here?
- Feedback on management/culture?

---

## 🚨 Firing Decisions

**When you need to fire someone:**
- Clear performance issues (documented)
- Repeated conversations; no improvement
- Role not fitting; despite support
- Cultural misfit affecting team
- Misconduct or integrity issues

**Process:**
1. **Document issues** — Specific, dated examples
2. **Warning conversations** — Be clear: "This needs to improve"
3. **Support/coaching** — Give them real chance to improve
4. **Final decision** — If no improvement, prepare for termination
5. **Execution** — HR involved; clear, respectful conversation
6. **Logistics** — Badge access, equipment, references, severance

**Hard truths:**
- Firing rarely comes as a complete surprise (if you've communicated well)
- It's hard; that's okay; do it with respect
- Keeping bad performers harms good ones
- Sometimes the job isn't right for the person; better to find right fit elsewhere

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Turnover is normal; manage it professionally
- Exit interviews teach you how to improve
- Clear performance standards prevent surprise terminations
- Support people genuinely before deciding to fire
- Firing done with dignity protects your reputation and their well-being
