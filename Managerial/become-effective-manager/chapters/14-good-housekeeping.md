# Chapter 14: Good Housekeeping
## *Department Communication and Culture*

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

**Your department's health depends on communication systems and culture.** Regular updates, clear processes, and intentional culture-building create cohesion and reduce chaos.

---

## 📢 Communication Systems

**Establish Regular Cadences:**
- Daily standup (optional, depending on team)
- Weekly team meeting
- Monthly department meeting
- Quarterly all-hands
- Annual offsite/retreat

**Regular communication:**
- Creates predictability
- Ensures alignment
- Builds relationships
- Reduces surprises and rumors

---

## 🤝 Team Meetings

**Well-run team meetings:**

| Element | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Standup** | 15 min; who's blocked; quick wins |
| **Retrospective** | What went well; what to improve |
| **Planning** | Roadmap, priorities, goals |
| **Culture/Social** | Team building, celebrations |

**Bad meetings:**
- No clear purpose
- Wrong people present
- No decisions made
- Ramble on without ending

---

## 🎯 Culture Building

**Intentional culture:**
- Values are explicit; live them
- Celebrate wins and effort
- Acknowledge mistakes without blame
- Build psychological safety
- Diversity and inclusion
- Work-life balance modeled

**Culture doesn't happen by accident:**
- It's shaped by what you pay attention to
- It's reflected in who you hire and fire
- It's lived in daily interactions
- It requires consistent reinforcement

---

## 📋 Documentation

**Team handbook/wiki should include:**
- Team mission and values
- How to join the team (onboarding)
- Processes and workflows
- Decision-making framework
- Communication norms
- How to escalate issues

---

## 💝 Recognition

**Celebrate success:**
- Public recognition (all-hands, emails)
- Private praise (1:1, notes)
- Bonuses and promotions
- Learning opportunities
- Autonomy and trust
- Time off and flexibility

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Communication systems reduce chaos
- Regular meetings create predictability
- Culture is built intentionally, not by accident
- Psychological safety enables excellence
- Recognition and celebration matter
- Diversity and inclusion are ongoing practices
