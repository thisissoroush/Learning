# Chapter 4: One-to-Ones
## *The Bedrock of Your Relationship with Staff*

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

**Weekly one-to-one meetings** are the foundation of every effective manager-employee relationship. These recurring, dedicated meetings create trust, surface problems early, and enable you to support your team effectively.

> **Key insight:** Skip one-to-ones and your team will feel neglected. Prioritize them and everything else becomes easier.

---

## ⏰ The Basics

**Schedule:** Every week, same time, same place (even if remote)  
**Duration:** 30-60 minutes  
**Frequency:** Non-negotiable; reschedule only if absolutely necessary  
**Privacy:** One person at a time; confidential space  

---

## 🤝 Contracting: Your First One-to-Ones

Your first 1:1 with someone is a **contracting** conversation. Set expectations and build rapport:

**Questions to Ask:**
1. Which areas would you like the most support with?
2. How would you like to receive feedback and support?
3. What could be a challenge of us working together?
4. How might we know if the support I'm offering isn't going well?
5. How confidential is the content of our meetings?

These answers create a working agreement and show you're invested in them.

---

## 💭 What to Talk About

**Keep 1:1s balanced:**

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Work Updates** | Current projects, progress, blockers |
| **Career Development** | Goals, skill growth, feedback |
| **Personal/Life** | General wellbeing, challenges (non-invasive) |
| **Feedback** | Both directions; what's going well and what needs work |
| **Challenges** | Frustrations, concerns, interpersonal issues |
| **Fun/Connection** | Build human relationship; get to know them |

**Good 1:1 Question Starters:**
- "How's work feeling this week?"
- "What's one thing that went well?"
- "What's been challenging?"
- "Do you have what you need to be successful?"
- "How are you feeling about your career progress?"
- "Is there anything I can help you with?"

---

## 📝 Taking Notes and Assigning Actions

During each 1:1:
- **Take Notes** — Shows you value the conversation; aids memory
- **Assign Actions** — Who does what by when
- **Follow Up** — Check on previous week's actions at start of next 1:1

**Action Format:**
- Clear owner (you or them)
- Clear deadline
- Clear description
- Reference in next week's 1:1

---

## ⚠️ You Are Not a Therapist

Know the boundaries:
- ✅ Support career growth, work challenges, interpersonal issues
- ❌ Don't provide therapy for mental health conditions
- If someone is struggling mentally, suggest professional help or EAP

**Boundaries Matter:** Distinguish between supporting someone and being their therapist.

## 📊 One-to-One Template

```
WEEKLY 1:1 AGENDA:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Opening (5 min)
├─ How are you?
└─ Quick personal check-in

Previous Actions (5 min)
├─ Did we complete what we said?
└─ Any blockers?

This Week (20 min)
├─ What's going well?
├─ What's challenging?
├─ Blockers or problems?
└─ Need support with anything?

Feedback & Development (15-20 min)
├─ How am I doing as your manager?
├─ Career growth/development?
└─ Feedback for them if needed

Next Steps (5 min)
├─ Confirm actions
└─ Address for next week
```

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Weekly 1:1s are non-negotiable
- Set up contracting conversation first
- Balance work, career, and personal/wellbeing topics
- Take notes; assign clear actions
- Use as early warning system for problems
- Your relationship quality depends on this
