# Chapter 11: Projects Are Hard
## *Managing Scope, Resources, and Expectations*

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

**Large projects fail when managers don't understand scope, resources, and reality.** Learn to estimate, manage expectations, and communicate clearly about what's actually possible.

---

## 👁️ The Eye of Sauron

**Beware excessive visibility on big projects:**
- Everyone watching increases pressure
- More visibility = more scrutiny
- High expectations = higher risk of disappointment
- Keep realistic visibility; under-promise, over-deliver

---

## 🎪 Victims of Your Own Success

**When you successfully deliver projects:**
- Expectations escalate
- Everyone wants you to do more
- Commitments increase
- Burnout risk increases

**Manage this:**
- Set realistic expectations early
- Don't overcommit
- Show the work/effort required
- Celebrate wins; recognize team effort

---

## ⚖️ Scope, Resources, and Time

**The Iron Triangle:**

```
         SCOPE
          /\
         /  \
        /    \
    TIME ---- RESOURCES

Can only optimize TWO:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Fast + Good = Expensive (many resources)
• Fast + Cheap = Bad (reduced scope)
• Good + Cheap = Slow (more time)

You can't have all three.
```

**When managing projects:**
- Clarify which two are most important
- Negotiate trade-offs explicitly
- Communicate constraints to stakeholders
- Monitor and adjust as reality emerges

---

## 📊 Estimation

**Why estimates fail:**
- Underestimating complexity
- Not accounting for dependencies
- Optimism bias (things always take longer)
- Lack of buffer for unknowns

**Better estimation:**
- Use historical data
- Add 20-30% buffer for unknowns
- Break into smaller pieces
- Re-estimate as you learn more
- Communicate uncertainty ("2-3 weeks" not "2 weeks")

---

## 📉 And Relax…

**After projects complete:**
- Retro: What went well? What to improve?
- Celebrate: Recognize team and individual efforts
- Document: Capture learnings
- Rest: Let team recover before next push
- Reflect: What would you do differently?

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Scope, resources, time: pick two
- Realistic estimates require buffer and historical data
- Communication about constraints prevents disappointment
- Celebrate wins; retro on learnings
- Manage visibility; don't over-commit
