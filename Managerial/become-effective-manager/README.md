# 📚 Become an Effective Software Engineering Manager
### *How to Be the Leader Your Development Team Needs*
**By James Stanier | The Pragmatic Bookshelf, 2020**

---

## 🎯 What This Book Is About

Most software engineers aren't prepared for management. This book gives you **practical, hands-on tools** to actually do the job on day one.

**James Stanier**, a hands-on engineering manager, covers:
- Your first week (building a snapshot of your team)
- Managing yourself (systems, organization, productivity)
- Core skills (1:1s, feedback, delegation, communication)
- People management (motivation, hiring, performance reviews)
- Organizational challenges (scaling, culture, remote work)
- Your future (career planning, dual ladders)

> **Core insight:** Management is a craft. You'll improve every day if you practice these skills.

## 📚 Quick Summary

**The Tools You Need:**
- Calendar (time-blocking)
- To-do list (task management)
- Email process (staying organized)

**Weekly Rhythm:**
- 1:1s with each report (60 min each)
- Team meeting (60 min)
- Planning/reflection (2 hours)

**Continuous Practices:**
- Give feedback weekly, specific, timely
- Gather information constantly
- Make thoughtful decisions
- Model the behavior you want
