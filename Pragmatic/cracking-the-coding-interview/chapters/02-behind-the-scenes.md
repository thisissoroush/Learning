# Chapter II — Behind the Scenes

> *"Each company has a slightly different interview style, but the fundamentals remain the same: they want to know if you can do the job."*  
> — Gayle Laakmann McDowell

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

Every major tech company runs a different hiring process — different stages, different decision-makers, different culture filters. Knowing what's behind the curtain at each company removes surprise and lets you tailor your preparation.

---

## 🏢 The Big Five — Company by Company

### 🔵 Microsoft

```
MICROSOFT PROCESS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Rounds:       4–5 interviews with engineers + manager
  Focus:        Problem solving + passion for tech
  Unique:       "As Appropriate" (AA) interview with
                senior engineer — carries veto power
  Culture fit:  "Do you care about technology?"
  Tip:          They want to see you solve it yourself.
                Hints given reluctantly. Work independently.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

### 🟠 Amazon

```
AMAZON PROCESS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Rounds:       4–5 interviews, includes Bar Raiser
  Focus:        Coding + Leadership Principles
  Unique:       Bar Raiser — senior interviewer whose
                job is to protect the hiring bar.
                Can veto regardless of other scores.
  Written:      Each interviewer focuses on 1–2 LPs
  Tip:          Prepare STAR stories for EVERY LP.
                Scale matters — they think BIG.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

**Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles (examples):**
- Customer Obsession
- Ownership
- Invent and Simplify
- Are Right, A Lot
- Deliver Results

> 💡 Amazon behavioral questions are NOT optional softballs — they carry as much weight as your coding rounds.

### 🔷 Google

```
GOOGLE PROCESS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Rounds:       4–6 interviews + Hiring Committee
  Focus:        Algorithms, data structures, scalability
  Unique:       Hiring Committee (HC) reviews ALL feedback.
                Your interviewers don't make the final call.
  Feedback:     Written up by each interviewer, scored 1–4
  Tip:          Google loves scalability + system design.
                Even for junior roles, think at scale.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

Google's 4-point scoring rubric:
```
1.0 = No Hire       (strong concerns)
2.0 = Lean No Hire  (some concerns)
3.0 = Lean Hire     (mild positives)
4.0 = Hire          (strong positives)
```

The HC looks for **at least one strong advocate** (4.0) combined with no strong opposition (1.0).

### 🔵 Apple

```
APPLE PROCESS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Rounds:       Many — can span multiple days, many teams
  Focus:        Technical excellence + passion for Apple
  Unique:       You interview with THE TEAM you'd join.
                Team members have significant influence.
  Culture:      They expect you to care about products.
                "Do you love Apple?" matters.
  Tip:          Know their products. Use them. Love them.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

### 🔴 Facebook (Meta)

```
FACEBOOK PROCESS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Rounds:       2 phone screens + 4–5 onsite
  Focus:        Coding + system design + behavioral
  Unique:       Moves FAST. Decisions within 1 week.
  Behavioral:   Called "Jedi" round — past experience +
                conflict resolution + leadership
  Tip:          They want builders. Show you ship things.
                Entrepreneurial mindset valued.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## 🔁 What Happens After Onsite — The Debrief

Most candidates imagine their interviewers huddle in a room and argue about you. The reality:

```
TYPICAL DEBRIEF FLOW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Each interviewer submits written feedback FIRST
     (before seeing others' feedback — prevents anchoring)

  2. Scores are revealed simultaneously to avoid bias

  3. Discussion focuses on outlier scores
     "Why did you give a 2 when everyone else gave a 3?"

  4. Hiring manager or committee makes final call

  KEY: Your interviewers advocate FOR you.
       Their reputation is tied to good hires.
       Give them ammunition to fight for you.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## 💡 Key Takeaways

| Company | Most Important Differentiator | Your Prep Focus |
|---------|-------------------------------|-----------------|
| Microsoft | Show independent problem solving | Work without hints |
| Amazon | Leadership Principles are real | Prepare 5+ STAR stories per LP |
| Google | Hiring Committee = consensus needed | Be consistent across all rounds |
| Apple | Team fit + product passion | Know their products deeply |
| Facebook/Meta | Speed + builder mindset | Show you ship, not just think |
